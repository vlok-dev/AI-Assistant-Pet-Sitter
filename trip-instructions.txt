=====================================================
  BUDGIE CARE INSTRUCTIONS
  Pet sitting: 2 budgies ("the babies")
  Starting Friday, for 2 weeks
=====================================================

Thank you so much for looking after our babies! Please read
through everything below. A lot of it we showed you in person,
and this is a reminder of how we do things.


-----------------------------------------------------
1. DOORS AND WINDOWS
-----------------------------------------------------

- The bathroom door is closed AT ALL TIMES.
- When showering, keep the bathroom door closed and open the
  bathroom windows so the steam can get out.
- Bedroom windows are closed AT ALL TIMES.
- The bedroom door: use your own judgement, depending on draft,
  cold, heat, etc.
    * If it is hot, have it more open.
    * If it is cold, have a good amount of openness, but
      still slightly closed.
    * It should always be at least slightly closed.
- The TV box we showed you must always sit in front of the
  bedroom door, blocking the entrance. If you know you will be
  moving in and out of the bedroom a lot, you can leave a gap
  so you can walk past, but otherwise the TV box must cover
  the entrance to the bedroom door.


-----------------------------------------------------
2. TEMPERATURE (THE BABIES' CAGE)
-----------------------------------------------------

Overall: use your judgement based on the weather and how it
feels. We don't want the babies too hot or too cold.

WHEN HOT
- Use your own judgement, but maybe put the blanket a little
  further back.
- Switch on one of the USB fans, set up as we showed you.

WHEN COLD
- Use your own judgement, but maybe close one of the windows
  in the bathroom.
- Make sure there is no massive draft coming into the bedroom.
  Maybe close the kitchen window a tad bit.
  ** NOT FULLY CLOSED. **
- The kitchen windows are only fully closed when you go to
  sleep.


-----------------------------------------------------
3. COOKING
-----------------------------------------------------

- When cooking, the bedroom door is ALWAYS closed.
- Try not to create a lot of smoke.


-----------------------------------------------------
4. CLEANING THE FOOD AND WATER BOWLS
-----------------------------------------------------

In simple terms:

 1. Take the food bowls out and take the water bowl out.
    ** MAKE SURE THE CAGE IS NOT OPEN. **
 2. Take the bowls to the bathroom, along with 2 paper towels:
      - Paper towel 1: lay it on the counter and put the
        bowls on it.
      - Paper towel 2: use it to dry the bowls once cleaned.
 3. Clean each bowl as we showed you, with a drop of soap in
    each one, then rinse thoroughly with HOT water.
    ** NO SOAP RESIDUE. **


-----------------------------------------------------
5. FEEDING AND REPLACING THE WATER
-----------------------------------------------------

Once the bowls are cleaned in the bathroom (see section 4):

 1. Take the food bowls and water bowl to the kitchen.
 2. Again, use 2 paper towels:
      - Paper towel 1: lay it on the kitchen counter.
      - Paper towel 2: use it to dry the pestle (the grey heavy
        stick we showed you, used for grinding the pellets).
 3. Before the morning feeding and water, make sure you wash
    and dry the pestle.
    ** THIS IS VERY IMPORTANT. **


-----------------------------------------------------
6. PAPER TOWELS IN THE CAGE (EVERY EVENING)
-----------------------------------------------------

Every evening, lay fresh paper towels in the cage:

- Take 5 sets of 2 (so 10 sheets in total).
- Remember how we showed you: break the paper towel into
  2-sheet pieces, 5 of them.
- Layout:
    * 2 on the right side
    * 2 on the left side
    * 2 in the left middle
    * 2 in the right middle
    * 2 in the centre middle
- A picture will also be shown for reference.

EVERY EVENING.


-----------------------------------------------------
7. HANDLING THE CAGE (IMPORTANT!)
-----------------------------------------------------

- Move your hands VERY SLOWLY inside the cage when changing
  their food, water and paper towels.
- Do not let them fly out. Open the cage door only enough to
  let your hands through, and block any other openings so they
  cannot escape.
- If they do get out, react quickly and open the windows, etc.


-----------------------------------------------------
8. GENERAL CLEANLINESS
-----------------------------------------------------

- NO chemicals on the floors, or anywhere for that matter.
- A sweep every now and then is more than fine. Tiny feathers
  collect under their cage.


=====================================================
Thank you again! The babies are in good hands.
